Her body is slim with a high, clearly shaped waist, long legs (legs about half her height or a little more) and shoulders that never look narrow.
The clothes sit on that body the way real fabric does: fitted and tucked pieces show the curve of the
waist and hips, the waistband sits at her real waist, and even a boxy or oversized outer hangs from her
shoulders with her slim figure visible inside it. Never a straight boxy column from chest to hip.
