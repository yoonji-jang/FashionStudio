The skirt keeps the exact fabric, sheen and pleats of its photos (no pleats if it has none) and is
drawn from its front side. It ends where it ends on the model (a midi skirt ends around the knee) and
keeps the worn silhouette: a straight or pencil skirt stays straight and narrow, an A-line or pleated
skirt opens gently. It has real volume, never flat. The top never hides the skirt's waist details.
