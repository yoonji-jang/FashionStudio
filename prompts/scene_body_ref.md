from the reference outfit photo, not from words: tucked in or loose (fully, front only or one side),
how many buttons are done up and how far it is closed, how the collar sits, how much of an inner layer

themselves (fit, volume, looseness, length, fabric) stay exactly as in their shop photos: wearing a
piece differently never makes it bigger, smaller, tighter or shorter. If the product cannot be worn the
reference's way (no buttons to close, too short to tuck), keep the product as it is and never add
buttons, a belt or anything it does not have.
