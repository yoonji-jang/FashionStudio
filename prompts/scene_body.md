Her body is slim with a high, clearly shaped waist, long legs (legs about half her height or a little
more) and shoulders that never look narrow.
The clothes sit on that body the way real fabric does: fitted and tucked pieces show the curve of the
waist and hips, the waistband sits at her real waist, and even a boxy or oversized outer hangs from her
shoulders with her slim figure visible inside it. Never a straight boxy column from chest to hip.
Each hem ends on her body where it ends on the shop model in the worn photos. A top with a front design
(wrap, ties, draping, peplum) hangs over the waistband so the design shows. Sleeves and trouser hems are folded or rolled up
only where the product photos show them folded. A layer worn underneath (tee, tank, shirt) shows only a
little at the neckline, cuffs and hem, and its hem peeks out just slightly below the outer piece, never
a hand's width.
