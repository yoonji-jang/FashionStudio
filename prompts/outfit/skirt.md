The skirt keeps the exact fabric, sheen and pleats of its photos (no pleats if it has none) and is
drawn from its front side. It ends exactly where it ends on the model in its photos, never longer, and
keeps the worn silhouette: a straight, pencil or H-line skirt stays straight and narrow (hem width close to
the hips), an A-line or pleated skirt opens gently. It has real volume, never flat. The top never hides the skirt's waist details.
